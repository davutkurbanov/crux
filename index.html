    Each game or lesson places topics on your map, and how well you know them. Recalling is how memory is built.

  2. 2It stops things fading.

    Left alone, most of what you learn is gone within a month. A two-minute review just before it goes brings it back, silver. Keep getting it right and it turns gold; hold gold, and it's fortified.
